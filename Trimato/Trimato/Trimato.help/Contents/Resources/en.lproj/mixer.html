Mix project audio

Choose Playback > Mixer or press Command-Shift-M. The Mixer contains Audio Tracks, Track Controls, and Project Playback. Changes apply to every clip on the selected track.

Choose a track

Select an audio track in Audio Tracks. Each item shows the track name, volume, and any active Mute or Solo setting. Left and Right Arrow select adjacent tracks while the track collection has focus. Return moves to Volume. Space plays or pauses the project.

Selecting a track leaves the playhead in place and keeps the current mix playing.

Adjust the track

  • Volume raises or lowers the whole track. Zero dB keeps its original level.
  • Mute silences the track during playback and export.
  • Solo plays only the tracks with Solo enabled. More than one track can be soloed. Solo does not change exports and clears when the Mixer closes.
  • Pan moves the track's sound toward the left or right. Center keeps its original position.
  • Stereo balance reduces one side of the stereo sound. Center keeps both sides at their original levels.
  • Stereo width narrows or widens the sound. Mono combines both sides, Original keeps the original width, and values above 100 percent widen the stereo sound.
  • Channel routing uses both channels, sends only the left or right source channel to both sides, or swaps the left and right channels.
  • Reset Track Mix restores Volume to zero dB, Pan and Stereo balance to Center, Stereo width to Original, and Channel routing to Both channels. It also clears Mute and Solo for that track.

Clip-level adjustments remain in place. Audio description ducking continues to reduce other project audio during description clips.

Play the project

Use the Project playhead slider to choose a playback position. Play and Pause control playback. Go to Beginning and Go to End move to the project boundaries. J, K, and L control backward playback, pause, and forward playback. Command-Left Arrow and Command-Right Arrow move between edit points; Command-Up Arrow and Command-Down Arrow go to the beginning and end.

Master Volume raises or lowers the combined project audio. Zero dB keeps the combined level unchanged.

Save and close

Mix adjustments take effect as you make them. Use Undo and Redo to reverse or restore changes. Press Command-S to save the project, including track adjustments, Mute settings, and Master Volume. These settings apply to exports. Press Command-W to close the Mixer and return to the project.
