Mix project audio

Mixer opens in a separate window for the current project. Resize or move it alongside the project window to give its controls more room. Command-Shift-M brings an open Mixer window forward.

Choose Playback > Mixer or press Command-Shift-M. The Mixer provides project playback, an Audio track picker, track adjustments, and Master Volume. Changes apply to every clip on the selected track.

Use Volume, Master Volume, and Mute with compatible Spatial Audio recordings. Choose High-quality Stereo in Export to include pan, balance, width, and channel routing changes. Read about audio export choices.

Project waveform and playback

Turn on Show Audio Waveforms in Settings > General to display an overview of the timeline audio. Use the Project playhead slider and timecode to choose a playback position.

Use Space to play or pause. Choose VoiceOver timecode feedback.

Loop an In–Out range

Loop follows the Project playhead slider. The Markers group contains Mark In, Mark Out, their timecodes, and Clear In and Clear Out. Move the playhead to the start of the range and choose Mark In or press I. Move to the end and choose Mark Out or press O. Turn on Loop, then play. Forward playback repeats this range while you adjust the mix. Loop is available when In is earlier than Out.

The In and Out values are shared with Editor and the export range. Clear In or Clear Out removes that boundary and turns Loop off. Closing Mixer turns Loop off and stops playback. Preparing a new playback preview also turns Loop off.

Choose a track

Choose a track with the Audio track picker. Command-Option-Up Arrow selects the previous audio track and Command-Option-Down Arrow selects the next. The shortcuts cycle through the audio tracks.

Selecting a track leaves the playhead in place and keeps the current mix playing.

Adjust the track

  • Volume raises or lowers the whole track. Zero dB keeps its original level.
  • Mute silences the track during playback and export.
  • Solo plays the tracks with Solo enabled for preview. You can solo several tracks. Closing Mixer clears Solo. Export uses the saved mix and Mute settings.
  • Pan moves the track's sound toward the left or right. Center keeps its original position.
  • Stereo balance reduces one side of the stereo sound. Center keeps both sides at their original levels.
  • Stereo width narrows or widens the sound. Mono combines both sides, Original keeps the original width, and values above 100 percent widen the stereo sound.
  • Channel routing uses both channels, sends only the left or right source channel to both sides, or swaps the left and right channels.
  • Reset Track Mix restores Volume to zero dB, Pan and Stereo balance to Center, Stereo width to Original, and Channel routing to Both channels. It also clears Mute and Solo for that track.

Clip-level adjustments remain in place. Audio description ducking continues to reduce other project audio during description clips.

Play the project

Use the Project playhead slider to choose a playback position. Play and Pause control playback. The frame-step buttons move one frame at a time, and the skip buttons move backward or forward by 10 seconds. Playing again after the project ends starts from the beginning. Go to Beginning and Go to End move to the project boundaries. J, K, and L control backward playback, pause, and forward playback. Command-Left Arrow and Command-Right Arrow move between edit points; Command-Up Arrow and Command-Down Arrow go to the beginning and end.

Master Volume raises or lowers the combined project audio. Zero dB keeps the combined level unchanged.

Save and close

Mix adjustments take effect during playback as you make them. Use Undo and Redo to reverse or restore changes. Press Command-S to save the project, including track adjustments, Mute settings, and Master Volume. These settings apply to exports. Choose Close, press Escape, or press Command-W to close Mixer and return to the project window. When you opened Mixer from a clip in Timeline Clips, closing returns to that clip.
