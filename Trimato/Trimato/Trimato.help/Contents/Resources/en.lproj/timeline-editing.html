Trimato Help.

Build and edit the project timeline

The Track picker chooses which track appears in Timeline Clips. Clips and transitions appear in playback order. Clips on a primary track stay together when you rearrange them. Additional tracks can contain clips at chosen times, with gaps between them.

Choose and manage a track

Use the Track picker or press Command-Option-Up Arrow and Command-Option-Down Arrow to change tracks. Track Actions adds, renames, moves, or deletes user-created video and audio tracks. Primary Video and Primary Audio are the project’s permanent tracks.

Choose magnetic or absolute placement

The Magnetic checkbox beside the Track picker controls the selected audio or video track. New additional tracks have Magnetic off. Clips on these tracks keep their assigned project times when another clip is edited, replaced, trimmed, deleted, pasted, or moved. Append adds a clip after the track’s last clip. Choose a space large enough for the clip between its neighbors.

Turning Magnetic on for a populated track asks for confirmation. Enabling it moves the first clip to project time zero and places every following clip directly after the previous clip, in chronological order. All gaps disappear. Subsequent edits can shift later clips on that track. Primary Video and Primary Audio remain magnetic.

Command-Z undoes the setting change and restores every original position together. Turning Magnetic off keeps the current positions. Use Undo to reverse an earlier magnetic edit.

New Describer and Voicer recordings use absolute tracks and retain their requested start times. If a previous recording track has been made magnetic, a new recording uses an available absolute track or creates one. Captions and description transcripts retain their own start and end times when the primary timeline changes.

Place an edited clip

Append to Timeline
Adds the clip after the destination track’s last clip.
Insert and Split
On a magnetic track, splits the clip beneath the project playhead, inserts the new clip between the two parts, and moves later clips forward. With Magnetic off, places the new clip in an available space at the playhead. After insertion, the project playhead moves to the incoming clip’s end so consecutive inserts remain in the order you make them.
Insert and Replace
Keeps the portion before the project playhead and replaces the remainder of that clip. Magnetic tracks shift later clips by the change in duration. With Magnetic off, later clips keep their exact times and an overlapping replacement is rejected.
Insert on Top with Source Audio
Shows and plays the inserted clip over the project for its duration.
Insert on Top over Primary Audio
Shows the inserted clip while the primary timeline audio continues.

Blade at the playhead

  1. Move the project playhead inside a timeline clip.
  2. Choose Blade at playhead or press Command-B while focus is in the Editor.

The two resulting instances receive lettered names such as Clip01 A and Clip01 B. The playhead remains at the new edit point.

Edit the active track in Editor

  1. Keep focus on any control in Editor and move the shared project playhead to the required time.
  2. Press Command-Option-Up Arrow or Command-Option-Down Arrow to select the required track and clip.
  3. Press Command-[ to trim that clip's start to the project playhead, or press Command-] to trim its end to the project playhead.
  4. On an additional track with Magnetic off, press plain [ to move the complete clip so its head begins at the playhead, or press plain ] to move it so its tail ends at the playhead.

The direct-edit clip is remembered separately for each track. If none has been remembered, Trimato uses the incoming clip at an edit point, a clip containing the playhead, the next clip, or the last earlier clip. Use Undo to reverse a trim.

Arrange timeline clips

  1. Choose a track and focus a clip in Timeline Clips.
  2. Press Space to pick up that clip.
  3. On magnetic tracks, press Left Arrow or Up Arrow to move it one clip earlier. Press Right Arrow or Down Arrow to move it one clip later. With Magnetic off, those arrows nudge it one project frame earlier or later instead.
  4. Press Space again, or Escape, to drop the clip. The list is reordered only when you drop it. Command-Z undoes the complete move.

To choose a destination, pick up the source clip with Space, focus the destination clip, and open its context menu with Control-Enter. Choose Move To, then Start, Before, After, End, or Playhead. Before and After use the focused destination; Start and End use the active track. Playhead places the clip at the project playhead on an additional track with Magnetic off.

Start, End, and Playhead also work directly on a focused clip. Use Timeline Clips with VoiceOver.

Press Enter or Return on a clip to open Cutting Board. In Timeline Clips, Space picks up or drops a clip. In Editor and Cutting Board, Space plays or pauses.

On additional tracks with Magnetic off, the arrows also nudge a focused clip directly. Left Arrow or Up Arrow moves it one frame earlier; Right Arrow or Down Arrow moves it one frame later. Linked audio moves with video. Remove attached transitions before moving a clip, and keep enough space between its neighbors.

The Timeline menu also provides Move Clip Earlier and Move Clip Later. These commands move by one clip on a magnetic track and one frame on an additional track with Magnetic off.

Current identifies the clip under the project playhead. Selected identifies a clip picked up with Space.

Press Command-C on a clip, focus a destination, then press Command-V to paste a copy after it. Command-Option-V moves the picked-up clip, or the copied clip, after the destination.

Video clips move between video tracks; audio clips move between audio tracks. Moving video carries its linked audio. Moving audio directly makes it independent of the picture. Choose a destination with enough space for the linked material and its transitions.

Trim a layered audio clip to the video ending

  1. In Editor, choose Go to End of Video or navigate to the required video edit point.
  2. Use Command-Option-Up Arrow or Command-Option-Down Arrow to select the audio track containing the clip.
  3. In Editor, press Command-] to trim the targeted clip’s end to the project playhead.

The clip ends at the project playhead. With Magnetic on, later clips on that track move earlier by the removed duration. With Magnetic off, they keep their assigned times. Use Undo to reverse the trim.

You can also focus the clip in Timeline Clips and choose Trim End to Project Playhead from its context menu.

Open a clip from Editor

Press C while focus is in Editor to open Cutting Board for the active-track clip at the playhead. At an edit point, Trimato opens the incoming clip. In a gap, it opens the next clip. In Timeline Clips, Enter or Return opens the focused clip regardless of the playhead position.

Rename a timeline item

  1. Focus a timeline clip or cutaway.
  2. Choose Rename Clip from the item’s context menu.
  3. Enter a unique timeline name, then choose Rename.

Trimato automatically gives repeated source names stable letter suffixes such as A and B. A custom name applies only to that timeline instance, is saved with the project, and can be undone.

Create and edit captions

Set In and Out around a passage in Editor, press Command-Shift-C, enter the text, and choose Add Caption. Choose Timeline > Captions > Finalize Captions to turn written passages into timed cues.

Choose Import Files to import SRT or WebVTT captions. Write, import, and finalize captions.

Add and edit transitions

Move the Editor playhead to a shared edit and press Command-T, or choose an adjoining clip in Timeline Clips and use the Transitions command.

F opens Quick Fade. X opens Quick Cross Dissolve for video or Quick Cross Fade for audio.

Choose transitions and prepare their source ranges.

Mute an audio track

Choose an audio track with the Track picker, then turn on Mute Track to silence it in preview and export. Turn it off to restore the sound. The setting is saved with the project and supports Undo.

Adjust an audio clip

Open an audio-track clip in Audio Cutting Board. Adjust its gain, equalizer, or filters, then choose Update Clip. Edit an audio clip.

Get information about a timeline item

Focus a clip or transition and press Command-I to open its Get Info window.

Set independent fade durations

In Quick Fade, select Fade In {clip name}, Fade Out {clip name}, or both, then enter each duration in seconds. Fade Audio includes the corresponding linked audio. Choose Apply Fade. Adjust fades and other transitions.

Additional video track fades change the clip's opacity, revealing the video underneath. Primary video fades reveal the black background when no picture is underneath.

Remove clips and delete media

Choose Delete Track from Track Actions to remove an additional track. If it contains content, review the confirmation and choose Delete Track or Cancel.

Choose Remove from Timeline in a clip’s context menu to remove that timeline clip and keep its source in Project Source. Choose Delete Media in Timeline Clips or Project Source to remove the source from the project, including all timeline clips and transitions that use it. The confirmation shows how many timeline clips use the source. Files remain in Finder. Use Undo to restore a removal or deletion. Save the project to keep your changes.

Show or hide captions

Select the Captions track in Timeline Clips, then turn Show captions on or off. This changes the caption overlay during playback. The caption cues remain available for editing. Choose Include captions separately when exporting.

Markers

Press M in Editor to add an absolute Marker, or Option-M to add a Chapter. Select the Markers track to navigate and edit its items in Timeline Clips. Enter edits the title and Marker or Chapter type; Delete removes the item.
