Video settings

Choose Trimato > Settings, then Video.

Preserve HDR

Turn on Preserve HDR to retain HDR brightness and color in compatible HEVC and ProRes exports. Turn it off to convert HDR to standard dynamic range, or SDR.

MP4 for smaller files (H.264) converts HDR to SDR automatically. H.264 exports from a standalone Cutting Board also convert automatically.

Choose an export format

In Export, choose Preserve Spatial Audio for compatible QuickTime output or High-quality Stereo for a stereo mix. Volume, mute, and supported audio transitions work with compatible spatial recordings.
