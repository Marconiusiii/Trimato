Trimato Manual contents.

Video settings

Choose Trimato > Settings, then Video.

Preserve HDR

Turn on Preserve HDR to keep HDR brightness and color when exporting HDR video as HEVC or ProRes. Turn it off to convert HDR video to standard dynamic range, or SDR. In Trim a Clip, choosing H.264 automatically converts to SDR regardless of this setting.

Export choices

Supported QuickTime exports preserve iPhone Spatial Audio and its stereo playback alternative. Compatible recordings support volume, mute, and audio transitions. Other audio effects and voice adjustments use a stereo preview and may require High-quality Stereo for export.

When you export Cinematic video to another format, you cannot change its focus afterward using the exported copy.

Choose export formats and review HDR, Spatial Audio, and Cinematic support.
