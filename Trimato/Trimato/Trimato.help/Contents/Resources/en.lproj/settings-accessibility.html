Trimato Help.

Accessibility settings

Choose Trimato > Settings, then Accessibility.

Interface text size

Set Interface Text Size from 100% to 200% to enlarge workspace text, including Project Source and Timeline Clips. Controls arrange themselves to fit the available space. Resize the window or adjust the pane dividers to give them more room.

Timecode feedback

Timecode Feedback controls playhead announcements with VoiceOver.

  • When stopped announces the time when you focus a paused playhead and after you finish adjusting it.
  • On Demand announces the time when you press T.

Press T in Editor, Mixer, or Cutting Board to hear the current time with either setting.

Show milliseconds

Show milliseconds starts on. It includes fractional seconds in displayed and spoken timecodes, In and Out points, marker positions, caption boundaries, and recording times. Turn it off to present whole seconds.

Edit positions retain their exact timing. Time fields accept fractional seconds with either setting.

Timecode style

  • Numeric uses minutes and seconds, such as 01:23.115. Hours appear from one hour onward, such as 01:04:12.344.
  • Time units uses names, such as 1 hour, 4 minutes, 12 seconds, 344 milliseconds.
  • Frames uses the frame number at the current frame rate.

Show milliseconds controls the fractional part of Numeric and Time units. Timecode style applies to readouts and requested playhead announcements. Editable time fields use numeric time. Jump to Time accepts a frame number with Frames selected, or hours, minutes, and seconds for the other styles.
