Accessibility settings

Choose Trimato > Settings, then Accessibility.

Timecode Feedback

  • Live provides timecode feedback as playback advances.
  • On Demand lets you request the timecode without continuous feedback.
  • Off disables automatic timecode feedback.

Press T in Editor or Mixer to hear the current timecode while playing or paused. In Clip Editor, T is available when On Demand is selected.

Timecode Verbosity

With Live or On Demand selected, choose Default, Short, or Frames. Default reads time units including milliseconds. Short uses a shorter time description. Frames reads the frame number.
