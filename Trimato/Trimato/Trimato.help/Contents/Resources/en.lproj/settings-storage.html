Trimato Help.

Storage settings

Choose Trimato > Settings, then Storage.

Media cache storage

Read Storage used, Cache files, Storage limit, and Stored in to review the cache. Choose Refresh storage usage to update the figures.

The cache contains frame indexes, audio waveforms, and compatible playback copies called proxies. It is stored in macOS Caches with a 10 GB limit.

Clear cached media

  • Clear cache not used recently removes analysis and proxies last used more than seven days ago.
  • Clear all cached media removes analysis and unneeded proxies, keeping active preparation and proxies required by open projects or editors.

Choose a cleanup command, then confirm it. Trimato recreates cleared analysis and proxies from the original media when needed.
