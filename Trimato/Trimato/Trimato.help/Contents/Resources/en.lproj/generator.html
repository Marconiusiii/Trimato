Trimato Help.

Create generated clips

Need another ingredient? Generator creates backgrounds, silence, and text clips for your project.

Press G in Editor or choose Timeline > Generator. Generator opens in the tool pane; Command-4 returns to the open tool.

Choose the content

  • Black creates a black picture.
  • Solid Color fills the picture with the chosen color.
  • Static Gradient blends First Color into Second Color in the selected Direction.
  • Silence creates a silent audio clip. Choose Mono or Stereo with Channels.
  • Text creates a title, lower third, caption, or subtitle as a video clip.

Set duration and placement

Choose Duration Units and enter the duration. Choose a Destination Track, or New Track and a name. The destination playhead gives the insertion time.

  • Append places the clip after the destination track’s last clip.
  • Insert and Split places it at the playhead, splitting and shifting material on a magnetic track.
  • Insert and Replace replaces material at the playhead.
  • Insert on Top in New Video Track places picture above the project video.

On a track with Magnetic off, placement needs enough space between neighboring clips. Review track placement.

Create text

Choose Text and a Template: Center Title, Title and Subtitle, Lower Third Center, Lower Third Left, Lower Third Right, Name and Role, Caption, or Subtitle.

Enter text in the multiline editor. Return starts a new line. Title and Subtitle and Name and Role have separate fields for the main and supporting text.

  • Typography: choose Font, Weight, Font Size in Points, Text Alignment, and Additional Line Spacing in Points. Fonts include System Sans, Rounded Sans, Serif, and Monospaced.
  • Appearance: choose Text Color, a Black or Transparent background, Outline, Shadow, and Text Backing Panel. Choose Custom in a color picker to enter a six-digit hexadecimal color.
  • Layout: set Screen Position, Safe Margin, Maximum Width, and offsets. Positive offsets move text right or down.

Expand a group to adjust its controls. Opening another detailed group closes the previous one. Text size scales with the video dimensions, and safe margins start enabled.

Choose Check Text Fit to review line count and fit within the safe area. Adjust wording, size, or layout to resolve text extending beyond that area. The check also reports small text and low contrast against a known opaque background. Review text over changing video in Editor.

Choose Transparent and Insert on Top in New Video Track to place text over video. A Text Backing Panel supplies color behind the lettering. Add fades using the transition tools.

Caption and Subtitle templates create individual text clips. For timed caption files, use Captioner or import SRT or WebVTT captions.

Update generated content

Generated items appear in the Generators folder in Project Source.

Open a generated timeline clip in Cutting Board and choose Edit Generator. Change the content or style, then choose Update Generator.

Choose Cancel, Escape, or Command-W to close the tool. For an unfinished draft, choose whether to discard it or continue editing. Adding or updating content closes the tool.
