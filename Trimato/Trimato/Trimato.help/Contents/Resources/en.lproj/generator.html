Trimato Help.

Create generated clips

Choose a Generator, set its duration, and choose the destination track. The destination playhead shows where insertion will begin.

Choose the content

  • Black creates a black picture with no audio.
  • Solid Color fills the picture with the selected color and has no audio.
  • Static Gradient blends First Color into Second Color in the selected Direction. It does not move and has no audio.
  • Silence creates an audio clip with no sound. Choose Mono or Stereo with Channels.
  • Text creates a title, lower third, caption, or subtitle. Choose a Template, enter the text, and adjust Typography, Appearance, and Layout. A transparent background lets underlying video show through.

Set the duration and destination

Choose Duration Units and enter the duration in the selected unit. Choose a compatible Destination Track, or choose New Track and enter its name.

Append adds the generated clip at the end of the destination track. Insert and Split places it at the playhead and splits existing material there. Insert and Overwrite replaces material at that position. Insert on Top in New Video Track places picture above the existing video.

Update generated content

Open an existing generated clip and choose Edit Generator. Adjust its content and choose Update Generator to keep the changes. Cancel leaves the existing content unchanged.
