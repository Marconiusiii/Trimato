Trimato quickstart guide

Welcome to Trimato! Here is a quick trip from an empty project to your first fresh cut.

Create a project

  1. Choose New Project in the welcome window.
  2. Enter a project name and choose Automatic from First Clip.
  3. Choose Next, select a location in Save Project, then choose Create.

Import clips

In Project, choose Import Files. Select the audio or video clips you want to use, then choose Open.

Select a clip in Project Source and choose Open Cutting Board from Actions.

Make the first cut

This is the bread and butter of Trimato. Or the bruschetta? Either way, this is where you shape an individual clip before adding it to your project.

  1. Use the Clip playhead slider to find the beginning of the section you want to keep, then press I to mark In.
  2. Move to the end of that section and press O to mark Out. Space plays or pauses; Left Arrow and Right Arrow make smaller adjustments.
  3. Press E to append the section to the project timeline.
  4. Press Command-W to close Cutting Board.

Open another source clip and repeat these steps. Each section joins the end of the timeline.

Arrange the clips

In Timeline, choose Primary Video for a video edit or Primary Audio for an audio edit with the Track picker.

  1. Focus a clip in Timeline Clips and press Space to pick it up.
  2. Use Left Arrow or Right Arrow to choose its position.
  3. Press Space again to drop it.

Add another ingredient

Once your clips are arranged, you can add transitions where they meet. Move the Editor playhead to the cut between two clips, then press Command-T to open Transitions in the Tool pane.

To add a title, background, or silent clip, choose Timeline > Generator. Generator opens in the Tool pane, where you choose the content and its placement in the project.

  • Add and edit transitions.
  • Create generated clips.

Review the edit

Press Command-2 to move to Editor. Press Home or Fn-Left Arrow to go to the beginning, then Space to play your assembled clips. Press Space again to pause.

To adjust a section, open its clip from Timeline Clips with Enter. Change In and Out in Cutting Board, then press Command-U to update the timeline clip.

Save and export

  1. Press Command-S to save the project.
  2. In Editor, press Option-I and Option-O to clear the project In and Out points for a complete export.
  3. Press Command-E or choose File > Export Project.
  4. Enter a filename, choose a location and format, then choose Export.

Open the exported file from its chosen location. Your first fresh cut is ready to serve!
