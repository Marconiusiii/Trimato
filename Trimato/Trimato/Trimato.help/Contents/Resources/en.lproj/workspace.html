Trimato Help.

Understand the workspace

Trimato brings your ingredients together in three panes: Project, Editor, and Timeline.

Project

Use Project Source to organize media in folders. Choose Import Files to add media, then use a clip’s Actions or context menu to open it in Cutting Board, place it on a track, or relink its source.

Editor

Use Editor to play the project, move the Project playhead, set In and Out points, and work at edit points.

The timecode button follows the playhead slider. Choose it to open Jump to Time, enter a time or frame number, then choose Jump.

Control playback and use markers.

Timeline

Choose a track with the Track picker. Timeline Clips lists its clips and transitions in playback order. Current identifies the clip under the project playhead. Selected identifies a clip picked up for movement.

Press Enter to open a focused clip in Cutting Board. Press Space to pick it up or drop it. On magnetic tracks, use the arrows to choose a picked-up clip’s position. On additional tracks with Magnetic off, arrows nudge a focused clip one project frame.

Additional tracks begin with Magnetic off, keeping clips at their assigned times. Turning Magnetic on asks for confirmation before packing the clips together from project time zero. Primary tracks stay magnetic.

Arrange clips and manage tracks.

Move to a pane

  • Command-1: Project Source, returning to its selected item or to Import Files in an empty project.
  • Command-2: Editor, at the Project playhead or its heading while playback is unavailable.
  • Command-3: Timeline, returning to the last focused item on the selected track.
  • Command-4: the open tool pane.

The Window menu provides these commands. Close any open dialog before returning to a project pane.

Tools

Generator, transition tools, Captioner, Describer, and Voicer open in the tool pane beside Editor. Use the divider to adjust its width.

Choose Cancel, press Escape, or press Command-W to close a tool. If it has unfinished changes, choose whether to discard them or continue editing. Adding or updating content closes the tool and returns to the originating control.

Mixer opens in a separate window. Press Command-Shift-M to open it or bring it forward.

Mix project audio.

Arrange the workspace

Choose Window > Portrait Video or press Command-L to switch layouts. Portrait mode places the picture beside the Editor controls when space allows. Landscape mode places the controls below the picture.

Resize the window and adjust the pane dividers to give the picture, Project Source, or Timeline more room. When Editor is narrow, its controls appear below the picture. Use the Mac’s full-screen command to enter or leave full screen.

Choose Settings > Accessibility > Interface Text Size to enlarge workspace text up to 200%.

Get Info

Focus a project source, Editor item, timeline clip, transition, or Cutting Board item and press Command-I. Get Info lists available details such as duration, container, codecs, encoder, resolution, and frame rate. Press Command-W to close it.

Appearance and sounds

Choose Settings > General to set Appearance, Accent color, Show Audio Waveforms, Processing sounds, and Export completion sound.

The accent highlights controls, waveforms, and selections. Selected clips also have a thicker outline; the playhead stays tomato red.
