Understand the workspace

The project workspace contains three main groups.

Project

Import source clips, create folders, open clips, add clips to the timeline, move clips between folders, and relink offline media.

Editor

Play the assembled project, move the project playhead, set In and Out points, move among edit points, blade the clip beneath the playhead, and check export progress.

Timeline

Choose a video or audio track with the Track picker, then review its clips and transitions in playback order. Current identifies the clip under the shared project playhead. Focusing a clip makes it the target for timeline commands and Get Info; Selected identifies only a clip picked up for movement.

Enter opens the focused clip in Clip Editor. Space picks it up or drops it. On magnetic tracks, arrows move a picked-up clip through the list. With Magnetic off on additional tracks, plain arrows nudge the focused or picked-up clip one project frame without overlapping neighboring clips. Mute Track silences the chosen audio track in preview and export. Split instances use lettered names such as Clip01 A and Clip01 B. Learn about placement, movement, and transitions.

Magnetic defaults to off for additional audio and video tracks. With it off, editing one clip leaves other clips at their assigned times. Enabling Magnetic asks for confirmation before moving the first clip to zero and packing the rest together without gaps. One Undo restores the setting and original positions. Primary tracks remain magnetic; captions and description transcripts remain independently timed.

Choose Move To > Playhead to place a focused or picked-up clip at the shared project playhead on an additional track with Magnetic off. Other clips keep their positions; an overlap blocks the move.

Move directly to a pane

Use the Window menu from anywhere in the active project window. Project Source (Command-1) returns to the selected Project Source item, or Import Files when the project has no media. Editor (Command-2) moves to the project playhead, or the Editor heading when playback is unavailable. Timeline (Command-3) returns to the last focused item on the active track, or Timeline Clips when there is no remembered item on that track.

These commands move keyboard and VoiceOver focus together. They do not move the playhead or open a clip. Close an open dialog before using them to return to a project pane.

Portrait Video layout

Choose Window > Portrait Video, or press Command-4, to switch between portrait and landscape modes. Portrait mode places the video on the left and the Editor controls on the right. Landscape mode places controls below the picture. The checked menu item indicates portrait mode, and your preference is saved. VoiceOver announces the mode when you switch.

When the Editor is too narrow for controls beside the picture, controls appear below it. Widen the window or give the Editor more width with the Project pane separator to restore the side-by-side arrangement. Drag the separator above Timeline to give the Editor more height.

The complete frame fits the available space without stretching. Project, Editor, and Timeline keep their names and navigation order, and Timeline Clips stays horizontal. The layout changes your workspace only; it does not change the video or exported project.

Get Info

Press Command-I to open Get Info for the focused project source, Editor, timeline clip, transition, or Clip Editor item. The title uses the item name, and every detail includes its label. Media details can include duration, container, video codec, audio codec, encoder, resolution, and frame rate. Close the window with Command-W or its standard window control.

Appearance

Choose Trimato > Settings > General. Set Appearance to System, Light, or Dark. System follows your Mac's appearance; Light and Dark keep Trimato in that appearance. The choice applies to all Trimato windows.

Choose Accent color to use Teal, Blue, Indigo, Purple, Rose, or Amber. The choice is saved and applies to open Trimato windows without restarting. Each color adapts to Light, Dark, and the Mac's Increase Contrast setting.

The accent highlights controls, waveforms, and Timeline selections. Selected clips also have a thicker outline. Playback position stays tomato red, and recording and error indicators retain their usual colors regardless of your accent choice.

Sound settings

Choose Trimato > Settings > General. Turn Processing sounds on or off for a soft ascending cue when an action you activate takes extra time, such as preparing a recording or filter preview. Moving between controls, automatic preview updates, immediate actions, loading screens, and export progress remain quiet.

Turn Export completion sound on or off to choose whether Trimato plays a short tune after a successful project export. These settings are separate from export notification permission.
