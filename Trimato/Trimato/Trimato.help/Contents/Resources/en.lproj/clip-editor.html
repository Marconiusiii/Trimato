Trimato Help.

Edit a clip

Use Cutting Board to mark ranges, remove sections, and adjust an individual clip.

Open the Cutting Board

  • Choose Trim a Clip in the welcome window, select an audio or video file, then choose Open.
  • Select a source clip and choose Open Cutting Board from Actions.
  • Choose a timeline clip or cutaway to open that instance in the Cutting Board.
  • With no project open, open an audio or video file with Trimato from Finder to edit it in a standalone Cutting Board. With a project open, the file is imported into that project.

Open a source clip from Project Source to start with its complete audio or video. Mark a range and append it to the timeline. When you close and reopen the source editor, those markers are cleared. Open a clip from Timeline Clips to restore that timeline instance’s saved range.

Continue after a standalone edit

  • Close the standalone Cutting Board window to return to the Trimato welcome window.
  • Choose Create Project from Clip, choose File > Create Project from Clip, or press Command-R to create a new project from the current edited ranges. Trimato closes the standalone editor and opens the new project workspace.

Edit audio

Clips opened from Primary Audio or another audio track use the Audio Cutting Board, including audio taken from a movie. Open the movie in Project Source or its clip on a video track to edit video.

Use the Clip playhead slider to choose a position. Left Arrow and Right Arrow move in 1/30-second steps and play a short audio preview. Shift-Left Arrow and Shift-Right Arrow move ten steps. Home and End move to the edit’s boundaries; Fn-Left Arrow and Fn-Right Arrow provide the same commands.

Turn on Show Audio Waveforms in Settings > General to display an audio overview.

Mark a range

Move the playhead to the range’s start and press I. Move to its end and press O. Option-I clears In; Option-O clears Out.

Delete or trim material

  • Choose Delete Selection or press Delete to remove the marked section.
  • Choose Edit > Trim Start to Playhead or press Command-[ to remove material before the playhead.
  • Choose Edit > Trim End from Playhead or press Command-] to remove material after the playhead.

Adjust a cut

Trim a little more, or bring a little back. When you delete material from inside a clip, the remaining sections meet at a numbered join: Edit Point 1, Edit Point 2, and so on. The joins are numbered from the beginning of the edited clip and renumbered as cuts are added or removed.

  1. Focus the Clip playhead. Press semicolon to move backward or apostrophe to move forward through edit points, In and Out points, and the clip’s beginning and end.
  2. At a numbered edit point, press Enter to open its adjustment sheet.
  3. Change In and Out using the original clip’s timecode. In marks the beginning of the deleted material; Out marks its end. With Frames selected in Settings, enter frame numbers instead.
  4. Choose Apply to update the cut and return to the adjusted join, or Cancel to dismiss the sheet.

Moving In later or Out earlier brings material back. Moving In earlier or Out later removes more.

In must be earlier than Out. Each value must fall within the original clip and leave some material on its side of the join. If a value needs correcting, read the message beside its field, correct the entry, and choose Apply. Your entries remain in the sheet while you make corrections.

Choose Edit > Undo to reverse an adjustment or Edit > Redo to reapply it.

Update an existing edit

When editing a timeline clip or cutaway from a project, choose Update Clip to apply the current edit to that timeline item.

Add the edit to the project

Use Append to Timeline, Insert and Split, or Insert and Replace with audio or video. Use Insert on Top to layer a video clip above the project picture. If both In and Out points are set, the placement uses that marked section. Otherwise, it uses the complete current edit.

Choose the timecode button immediately after the playhead slider and before Playback to open Jump to Time. Enter a frame number when Frames is selected in Settings, or enter hours, minutes, and seconds for other formats. Jump moves the playhead and focuses it. Cancel returns focus to the timecode button.

Playback normally continues beyond your In and Out points. Select Play In–Out Range in Playback to audition only the marked section. Clear either point with Option-I or Option-O, including immediately after appending a clip with E.

When a video clip contains audio, use the Audio Only menu to append its audio to a track, insert it at the project playhead, or insert and replace. Add Audio Only to Track lists audio tracks and can create a named audio track. The new clip is placed on an audio track.

Use New Track from Video or New Track from Audio to create a named track and append the current edit. The same commands are available from a source clip's Actions menu and context menu in Project Source.

Press Option-E to open Add to Track. Choose a track, then choose Append to Selected Track. To create a track, enter a New Track Name and choose Create Audio Track and Append or Create Video Track and Append.

Adjust recorded narration

For Audio Description and Voicer recordings, open the Voice tab to match loudness, even out levels, or adjust Voice level. Play with Primary Audio reviews the draft in context. Choose Update Clip to save it, or Apply voice settings to the named track to update existing narration on that track.

Adjust recorded narration.

Add Filter also offers Match voice loudness to Primary Audio and Even out voice for recorded narration. Use Play preview and Bypass filter to compare the adjustment, then choose Add or Apply. Manage these adjustments in Applied Filters.

Adjust the equalizer

Use Bass, Midrange, and Treble, or choose an EQ preset: Flat, Warmer voice, Clearer speech, or Less bass. Reduce low rumble and Reduce high-frequency hiss reveal cutoff sliders. Gain is in the Audio tab. Choose Update Clip to save the adjustments.

With Play In–Out Range enabled, reaching Out ends playback. Press Play or Space to start again from In. Pausing within the range resumes from the paused position.

Apply clip filters

Match Loudness adjusts the retained edit toward your chosen Playback loudness. It keeps the result quieter if reaching that target would exceed the selected Peak ceiling. Later effects, clip volume, and track mixing can change the final level. Use Limit loud peaks to limit the clip after its other filters, and listen to the complete mix before exporting.

Choose Add Filter to select and adjust an effect. Preview the edited clip, then choose Update Clip to save a timeline instance.

Choose and adjust clip filters.

Open a filter’s context menu and choose Edit Filter to change it, Enable Filter or Disable Filter to switch its effect on or off, or Remove Filter to remove it. Use Reset in the filter editor to restore its starting values. Enable Filter restores a disabled filter with its saved settings.

Trim silences

Use Trim Silences in Audio Cutting Board or Video Cutting Board to shorten pauses.

Trim silences from a clip.

Export

The Export section at the bottom contains Export Clip, Update Clip, and Add to Timeline. Export Clip saves a separate media file. Update Clip saves edits to the timeline clip and appears when editing a timeline clip. Add to Timeline places the edited media in the project.

Use playback and editing keys with VoiceOver.
