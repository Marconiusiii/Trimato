Keyboard shortcuts

Playback and navigation

Audio and video clips use the same playback and navigation commands. In an audio Clip Editor, Left Arrow and Right Arrow use 1/30-second editing steps and provide audible scrub feedback.

ShortcutCommand
SpacePlay or pause
JPlay backward; press again to increase speed
KPlay or pause
LPlay forward; press again to increase speed
Left ArrowMove backward one frame
Right ArrowMove forward one frame
Command-Left ArrowPrevious marker or edit point, announcing video, audio, or both
Command-Right ArrowNext marker or edit point, announcing video, audio, or both
Command-Up ArrowGo to beginning
Command-Down ArrowGo to end

Markers and Clip Editor editing

ShortcutCommand
ISet or replace the In point
OSet or replace the Out point
DeleteDelete the marked section in Clip Editor or the selected project timeline item
Command-[Trim the source edit start to the Clip Editor playhead
Command-]Trim the source edit end to the Clip Editor playhead
Command-UUpdate the current timeline clip or cutaway edit

Project timeline

ShortcutCommand
Command-BBlade at the project playhead
Command-TOpen Add Transition for the clip at the Editor playhead or the focused Timeline clips item
Command-[While focus is in Editor, trim the start of the active track's announced clip to the shared project playhead
Command-]While focus is in Editor, trim the end of the active track's announced clip to the shared project playhead; when a Timeline clip is focused, trim that focused clip's end instead
[While focus is in Editor, move the active additional-track clip so its head begins at the shared project playhead without trimming its stored source
]While focus is in Editor, move the active additional-track clip so its tail ends at the shared project playhead without trimming its stored source; source positioned before project time zero is hidden non-destructively
COpen Clip Editor for the active-track clip at or immediately after the Editor playhead
Command-CCopy the focused Timeline clip
Command-VPaste a copy after the focused Timeline clip
Command-Option-VMove the copied Timeline clip after the focused Timeline clip

Option-Command-Up ArrowSelect the previous timeline track and announce its direct-edit clip without moving VoiceOver focus from the Editor
Option-Command-Down ArrowSelect the next timeline track and announce its direct-edit clip without moving VoiceOver focus from the Editor
Option-Command-Left ArrowMove the selected timeline clip earlier
Option-Command-Right ArrowMove the selected timeline clip later
XOpen Quick Cross Dissolve or Quick Cross Fade for the edit at the Editor playhead
FOpen Quick Fade for the clip at the Editor playhead

Place a clip

Append, Insert and Split, and Insert and Overwrite accept audio or video. Insert on Top requires a video source.

ShortcutCommand
EAppend to Timeline
WInsert and Split
DInsert and Overwrite
QInsert on Top with Source Audio
Option-QInsert on Top over Primary Audio
