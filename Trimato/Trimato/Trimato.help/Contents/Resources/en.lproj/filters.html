Add and adjust clip filters

Choose a filter in Add Filter, adjust its controls, and choose Add to keep it in the clip draft. In Applied Filters, use a filter's context menu to edit, enable, disable, or remove it. Disabling preserves its settings.

In Edit Filter, Apply keeps the adjusted settings. Reset restores the filter's starting values. Cancel discards the filter editor’s draft. Choose Update Clip in a timeline clip's editor to save the draft to the project.

Filter choices

Brightness and Contrast
Adjust overall lightness and the difference between dark and bright areas.
Color Adjustment
Adjust color intensity, warmth, and green or magenta tint.
Black and White
Remove color while preserving differences in brightness.
Bleach Bypass
Reduce color saturation and deepen shadows and contrast. Increase Amount for a stronger effect. Zero uses the original picture.
Technicolor
Adjust Effect strength to blend the color treatment with the original image. Zero uses the original picture. Cyan horizontal offset, Magenta horizontal offset, and Yellow horizontal offset move each color up to 20 pixels left or right, creating colored fringes around edges. Each starts Centered. Reset centers the offsets and restores Effect strength to 50 percent.
Sharpen
Emphasize edges. Strong settings can add visible outlines.
Reduce Video Noise
Soften fine image noise. Strong settings can remove detail.
Crop and Orientation
Remove pixels from the edges, then rotate or flip the image. The result fits the project frame.
Equalizer
Adjust low, middle, and high frequencies or reduce rumble and hiss.
Reduce Background Noise
Reduce steady background noise. Strong reduction can affect speech.
Even Out Volume
Compress louder passages and limit peaks to reduce changes in volume.
Match Loudness
Adjust the retained edit toward a target perceived loudness, measured in LUFS.
Reverb
Add the sound of a room. The effect ends at the clip’s Out point.
Plate Reverb
Add a smooth, bright reverberation to speech, music, or other audio. Amount blends the original sound with the reverb: zero keeps the original, and 100 percent plays only the reverb. Reverb length in seconds sets an approximate fade time from 0.2 to 6 seconds. Brightness ranges from a subdued tail to a brighter one. Reset restores Amount to 25 percent, Reverb length to 1.8 seconds, and Brightness to 50 percent. The tail stops at the clip’s Out point.
Echo
Add a delayed repeat. The effect ends at the clip’s Out point.
Soften harsh S sounds
Reduce sharp S and sh sounds in speech.
Limit loud peaks
Keep sudden loud peaks below the selected level.

Preview and compare

Review the processed picture and sound in Cutting Board before updating the timeline clip.

If a preview fails, use Preview Recovery to retry it or revert the filter changes.

Where Play preview is available, use it to hear the draft before applying it. Bypass filter switches between the original and adjusted sound. Play with Primary Audio includes the surrounding project audio. Stop preview ends playback.

Loudness and peaks

Match Loudness works on the retained edit. Playback loudness sets the target in LUFS. Peak ceiling keeps the result quieter when reaching that target would exceed the ceiling. Later effects and mixing can change the final level. Listen to the complete mix before exporting.

Adjust recorded narration

For Audio Description and Voicer recordings, use the Voice tab in Audio Cutting Board. Set dialogue reference In and Out, choose Play reference, then choose Match voice loudness to Primary Audio.

Even out voice reduces level differences between words; Smoothing amount sets its strength. Voice level adjusts the overall level. Use Play with Primary Audio to review narration in context.

Choose Update Clip to apply the draft to this clip. Apply voice settings to the named track changes the voice settings of existing recorded clips on that track.

Record with Voicer and Describer.

Add Filter also offers Match voice loudness to Primary Audio and Even out voice for recorded narration. Preview and compare the adjustment, then choose Add or Apply. Manage it through the Applied Filters context menu.
