Add and adjust clip filters

Choose a filter in Add Filter, adjust its controls, and choose Add to keep it in the clip draft. In Applied Filters, use a filter's context menu to edit, enable, disable, or remove it. Disabling preserves its settings.

In Edit Filter, Apply keeps the adjusted settings. Reset restores the filter's starting values. Cancel leaves the draft unchanged. Choose Update Clip in a timeline clip's editor to save the draft to the project.

Filter choices

Brightness and Contrast
Adjust overall lightness and the difference between dark and bright areas.
Color Adjustment
Adjust color intensity, warmth, and green or magenta tint.
Black and White
Remove color while preserving differences in brightness.
Sharpen
Emphasize edges. Strong settings can add visible outlines.
Reduce Video Noise
Soften fine image noise. Strong settings can remove detail.
Crop and Orientation
Remove pixels from the edges, then rotate or flip the image. The result fits the project frame.
Equalizer
Adjust low, middle, and high frequencies or reduce rumble and hiss.
Reduce Background Noise
Reduce steady background noise. Strong reduction can affect speech.
Even Out Volume
Compress louder passages and limit peaks to reduce changes in volume.
Match Loudness
Adjust the retained edit toward a target perceived loudness, measured in LUFS.
Reverb
Add the sound of a room. The effect ends at the clip’s Out point.
Echo
Add a delayed repeat. The effect ends at the clip’s Out point.
Soften harsh S sounds
Reduce sharp S and sh sounds in speech.
Limit loud peaks
Keep sudden loud peaks below the selected level.

Preview and compare

Where Play preview is available, use it to hear the draft before applying it. Bypass filter lets you compare the sound without the adjustment. Play with Primary Audio includes the surrounding project audio. Stop preview ends playback.

Loudness and peaks

Match Loudness works on the retained edit. Playback loudness sets the target in LUFS. Peak ceiling keeps the result quieter when reaching that target would exceed the ceiling. Later effects and mixing can change the final level. Listen to the complete mix before exporting.

Adjust recorded narration

Audio Description and Voicer recordings have a Voice tab. Set the dialogue reference In and Out times, audition it with Play reference, and choose Match voice loudness to Primary Audio. Matching adjusts loudness, not microphone tone or room sound. Use Even out voice with Smoothing amount to reduce differences between louder and quieter words. Voice level adjusts the overall level. Play with Primary Audio previews the draft at its project position, including description ducking.

Choose Update Clip to apply the draft to this clip. Apply voice settings to the named track changes the voice settings of existing recorded clips on that track. Other filters and clip edits are preserved. See Record with Voicer and Describer for the recording workflow.

Add Filter also offers Match voice loudness to Primary Audio and Even out voice for recorded AD and Voicer clips. Match voice loudness to Primary Audio includes the dialogue-reference controls. Choose Add to keep the adjustment in the clip draft. Use Play preview and the Bypass filter switch to compare the draft before choosing Add or Apply. Play with Primary Audio includes the surrounding project audio. Stop preview ends playback; Cancel leaves the draft unchanged. Both adjustments appear in Applied Filters, where their context menus offer Enable Filter or Disable Filter, Edit Filter, and Remove Filter. Disabling an adjustment preserves its settings.
