Control playback and use In and Out points

Use these playback commands in Editor or Cutting Board. In Timeline Clips, Space picks up or drops a clip and arrows control its placement.

Arrange timeline clips.

Play and pause

  • Space or K: play or pause.
  • J: play backward. Press again to increase reverse speed.
  • L: play forward. Press again to increase forward speed.

Choose a position

  • Left Arrow or Right Arrow: move one frame. In Audio Cutting Board, move 1/30 second with a short audio preview.
  • Shift-Arrow: move ten frames or audio steps. Hold to repeat.
  • Skip back 10 seconds or Skip forward 10 seconds: move by ten seconds.
  • Command-Left Arrow or semicolon: previous edit point.
  • Command-Right Arrow or apostrophe: next edit point.
  • Home, Fn-Left Arrow, or Command-Up Arrow: go to the beginning.
  • End, Fn-Right Arrow, or Command-Down Arrow: go to the end.

In Editor, Command-Option-Up Arrow and Command-Option-Down Arrow choose the track for edit-point navigation. Choose the Markers track to navigate marker times.

Jump to a time or frame

  1. Choose the timecode button after the playhead slider in Editor or Cutting Board.
  2. In Jump to Time, enter hours, minutes, and seconds separated by colons. Fractional seconds are accepted. With Frames selected in Settings, enter a frame number starting from zero.
  3. Choose Jump or press Return.

Playback pauses and the playhead moves to the chosen position. Cancel or Escape returns to the timecode button.

If Could Not Jump appears, read the allowed range, choose OK, and correct the entry.

Choose Timecode style and Show milliseconds in Settings > Accessibility to set the displayed format.

Choose VoiceOver timecode feedback.

Set In and Out

  • I or Mark In sets the In point at the playhead.
  • O or Mark Out sets the Out point.
  • Option-I or Clear In clears In.
  • Option-O or Clear Out clears Out.

Set In before Out to choose a range for placement or export. Clear both to use the complete edit or project.

Review a Cutting Board range

Choose Play In–Out Range in Playback to play from the marked section and stop at Out. Clear the checkbox to play through the complete edit. Clearing either marker also turns range playback off.

After appending a section with E, clear its points or mark another range to continue editing the source.

Create Markers and Chapters

Press M in Editor to create a Marker at the playhead, or Option-M to create a Chapter. Playback continues. The first entry creates the Markers track.

Markers and Chapters keep their assigned project times when clips change. Select the Markers track and press Enter on an entry to edit its Title and Type. Press Delete to remove it.

With the Markers track selected, Command-Left Arrow and Command-Right Arrow navigate its entries. Enter edits the entry at the playhead.

Marker audio in Settings > General plays a short note when an entry is created. Turn it off for silent creation.
