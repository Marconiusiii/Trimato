Control playback and use In and Out points

The playback commands on this page apply while focus is in Editor or Clip Editor. In Timeline Clips, Space picks up or drops a clip, and plain arrows reorder a picked-up primary-track clip or nudge an additional-track clip. Read about Timeline clip movement.

Play and pause

  • Press Space or K to play or pause.
  • Press J to play backward. Press J again to increase reverse speed.
  • Press L to play forward. Press L again to increase forward speed.

Move through the video

  • Press Left Arrow or Right Arrow to move one frame.
  • Hold Left Arrow or Right Arrow for continuous movement.
  • Activate Skip back 10 seconds or Skip forward 10 seconds.
  • Press Command-Left Arrow or Command-Right Arrow to move among markers and edit points.
  • Press Command-Up Arrow to go to the beginning.
  • Press Command-Down Arrow to go to the end.

Use the time display

  • Activate the time display to switch between timecode and frame number.
  • In a project Editor, press C to open Clip Editor for the active-track clip at or immediately after the playhead.

Set and clear points

  • Activate Mark In or press I to set or replace the In point.
  • Activate Mark Out or press O to set or replace the Out point.
  • Activate Clear In or Clear Out to remove a point.

Use points for export

  • Set both In and Out to export that range.
  • Clear both points to export the complete clip or project.
  • Place In earlier than Out.
