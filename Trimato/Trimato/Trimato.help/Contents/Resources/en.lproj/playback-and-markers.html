Control playback and use In and Out points

The playback commands on this page apply while focus is in Editor or Clip Editor. In Timeline Clips, Space picks up or drops a clip, and plain arrows reorder a picked-up clip on a magnetic track or nudge an additional-track clip with Magnetic off. Read about Timeline clip movement.

Play and pause

  • Press Space or K to play or pause.
  • Press J to play backward. Press J again to increase reverse speed.
  • Press L to play forward. Press L again to increase forward speed.

Move through the video

  • Press Left Arrow or Right Arrow to move one frame.
  • Hold Left Arrow or Right Arrow for continuous movement.
  • Activate Skip back 10 seconds or Skip forward 10 seconds.
  • Press Command-Left Arrow or Command-Right Arrow to move among markers and edit points.
  • Press Command-Up Arrow to go to the beginning.
  • Press Command-Down Arrow to go to the end.

Use the time display

  • Activate the time display to switch between timecode and frame number.
  • Choose Trimato > Settings > Accessibility to set Timecode Feedback to Live, On Demand, or Off.
  • With Live or On Demand selected, choose Default, Short, or Frames from Timecode Verbosity.
  • Press T in Editor or Mixer to hear the current timecode with any feedback setting. In Clip Editor, select On Demand to use T.
  • In a project Editor, press C to open Clip Editor for the active-track clip at or immediately after the playhead.

The playhead’s VoiceOver timecode stays stable during continuous playback. Pausing or completing a seek or frame jog updates it to the stopped position without requiring a focus change. The visible timecode continues to advance during playback.

Set and clear points

  • Activate Mark In or press I to set or replace the In point.
  • Activate Mark Out or press O to set or replace the Out point.
  • Activate Clear In or press Option-I to remove the In point.
  • Activate Clear Out or press Option-O to remove the Out point.

Use points for export

  • Set both In and Out to export that range.
  • Clear both points to export the complete clip or project.
  • Place In earlier than Out.

Create markers during playback

In the project Editor, press semicolon (;) to create a marker at the current playhead position. Playback continues. The first marker creates the Markers track and displays it in Timeline Clips. New markers are named Marker 1, Marker 2, and so on.

Markers keep their absolute project times when clips are moved, trimmed, or deleted. The Markers track is never magnetic. Diamond indicators identify markers above the playhead slider; bookmark indicators identify chapters.

Select the Markers track and use Command-Left Arrow and Command-Right Arrow in Editor to navigate its marker times. With a marker at the playhead, press Enter to edit its Title and Type, or Delete to remove it. Enter on a marker in Timeline Clips also opens the editor. Choose Marker for an editing cue or Chapter for a chapter boundary. Marker changes support Undo and Redo.

Marker audio in General settings plays a short note when a marker is created. Turn it off to create markers silently. The note is not included in exports and is suppressed during recording.
