Create and edit captions

Create and edit captions on the Captions track.

Set In before Out to choose the passage for a new caption.

  1. Set the project In point at the beginning of a spoken passage and the Out point at its end.
  2. Press Command-Shift-C or choose Timeline > Captions > New Caption.
  3. Write the complete passage as spoken. Choose Insert Caption Description to add a music description, sound description, or lyrics marks.
  4. Choose Play Selection to hear the marked section and watch it in the main Editor.
  5. Choose Add Caption.

Add Caption places the draft at the marked times and clears In and Out. Repeat for each passage.

Edit a caption

Choose a caption in Timeline Clips to open it in Captioner. Edit its text or times, then update it. Its context menu also provides commands to move the playhead to the caption or delete it.

Captioner opens in the tool pane. Press Command-4 to return to it. Choose Cancel, press Escape, or choose File > Close Captioner to close it. For an unfinished draft, choose whether to discard it or continue editing.

Finalize written captions

  1. Finish marking and writing the spoken passages you want to caption.
  2. Choose Timeline > Captions > Finalize Captions.
  3. Review any passage Trimato leaves as a draft, adjust its text or marked times, and finalize again.

Finalizing divides written passages into cues of up to two lines, preserving words, punctuation, and entered line breaks.

Cues use a reading rate of 160 words per minute and durations from 40 project frames through six seconds. Available space after the marked Out point can provide extra reading time, up to the next caption or the project end.

The results identify passages that need timing or text adjustments. Correct those passages and finalize again. Use Undo to reverse finalization in one step.

Import a caption file

Choose Import Files and select an SRT or WebVTT file. Imported cues join the Captions track at their supplied project times, ready for editing and export.

Include captions in an export or save a caption file.
