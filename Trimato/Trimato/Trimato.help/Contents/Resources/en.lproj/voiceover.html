Trimato Help.

Use Trimato with VoiceOver

Move through the workspace

The Window menu provides shortcuts that move keyboard and VoiceOver focus together in the active project:

  • Command-1 moves to Project Source.
  • Command-2 moves to Editor.
  • Command-3 moves to Timeline.
  • Command-4 moves to the open tool pane.

Press Escape or Command-W to close a tool and return to the originating control. Close an open dialog before moving to a project pane.

Play and edit

In Editor or Cutting Board, use Space, J, K, and L for playback. Use Left Arrow and Right Arrow for frame steps, Shift-Arrow for ten-frame jumps, and Home or End for the timeline boundaries.

If Quick Nav uses the plain arrow keys, turn it off for frame stepping or clip movement. The Playback menu provides navigation commands, and timeline context menus provide placement commands.

Use Command-Left Arrow and Command-Right Arrow, or semicolon and apostrophe, to move through edit points. Trimato announces the incoming clip at a shared cut, the ending clip before a gap, or the caption or marker at that position.

Command-Option-Up Arrow and Command-Option-Down Arrow select a track and announce the clip targeted for direct editing. Command-[ trims that clip’s start to the playhead; Command-] trims its end. Press C to open the active-track clip at or immediately after the playhead in Cutting Board.

Review track selection and direct editing.

Choose timecode feedback

In Settings > Accessibility, set Timecode Feedback to When stopped or On Demand. When stopped announces the time when you focus a paused playhead and after adjustments finish. On Demand announces it when you press T.

Press T in Editor, Mixer, or Cutting Board to hear the current time with either setting. Choose Timecode style and Show milliseconds to set its format.

In Cutting Board, Clip Ready announces when preparation has finished. The Clip playhead is the starting control.

Work in Timeline Clips

Choose a track, then navigate its clips and transitions. Current identifies the clip under the project playhead. Selected identifies a clip picked up for movement.

Press Enter or Return to open the focused clip in Cutting Board, or activate a transition to edit it.

  1. Press Space on a clip to pick it up. Trimato announces its name and position.
  2. On a magnetic track, use Left Arrow or Up Arrow to move it earlier, or Right Arrow or Down Arrow to move it later.
  3. Press Space or Escape to drop it at the chosen position. Command-Z undoes the move.

On an additional track with Magnetic off, the arrows nudge a focused clip one project frame. Trimato announces its new start frame. Remove attached transitions before moving the clip and keep enough space between neighboring clips.

To choose a specific destination, pick up the source clip, navigate to the destination, and open its context menu with Control-Enter or VO-Shift-M. Choose Move To, then Start, Before, After, End, or Playhead. Playhead is available on additional tracks with Magnetic off.

Command-C copies the focused clip. Command-V pastes a copy after the destination. Command-Option-V moves the picked-up clip, or the copied clip, after the destination.

Get information

Focus an item and press Command-I. VoiceOver starts on its Info heading. Press Command-W to close Get Info.

Inspect a video frame

The Video frame image precedes the Project playhead slider in Editor when video preview is available. Its value gives the project time and frame number. Press VO-Shift-L to request an image description from macOS.
