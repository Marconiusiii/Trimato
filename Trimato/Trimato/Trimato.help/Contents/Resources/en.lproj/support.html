Trimato Help.

Support Trimato

Trimato is free. Optional contributions support future updates.

Choose an amount

  1. Choose Support Trimato from the Trimato menu or the welcome window.
  2. Choose A little slice, A juicy cut, A fresh take, or The whole tomato. Each button shows its App Store price in your currency.
  3. Confirm the amount in the App Store payment sheet.

The Thank you sheet includes your chosen option and its date. Choose Done to return to Support Trimato. The latest thank-you is saved on this Mac and appears when you reopen the window.

Each option is a one-time contribution. You can choose the same amount again whenever you wish.

Choose Close, press Escape, or press Command-W to close Support Trimato.

Complete a pending payment

If the App Store requires approval or another payment action, Purchase pending shows Waiting for purchase completion. Complete the request through the App Store. When it finishes successfully, Trimato displays Thank you. Choose Done to return to Support Trimato.

Choose Close to leave the pending sheet while waiting for approval.

Load prices

Prices and payments require an App Store connection. If loading fails, check your connection and choose Retry.

Use your App Store purchase history to review charges or request assistance.
