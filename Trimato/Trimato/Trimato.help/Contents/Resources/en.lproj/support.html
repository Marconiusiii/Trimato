Support Trimato

Trimato is free. All editing features are available without a purchase. You can support the developer with an optional tip.

Make a support purchase

  1. Choose Support Trimato from the Trimato menu, or choose Support Trimato in the welcome window.
  2. Choose A little slice, A juicy cut, A fresh take, or The whole tomato. Each button shows its App Store price in your currency.
  3. Confirm the purchase in the App Store payment sheet.

After a verified purchase, a Thank you sheet names your selected option. Choose Done to return to Support Trimato. The latest thank-you remains visible in the window and is available on this Mac when you reopen it.

Each option is a one-time consumable purchase. You can buy the same option again. Support purchases do not start a subscription or unlock features, and each option receives the same thank-you.

Choose Close, press Escape, or press Command+W to close the support window.

Pending purchases and connection problems

If a purchase needs approval or another payment action, a Purchase pending sheet explains its status. Complete the request from the App Store. The Thank you sheet appears when the purchase is verified.

Loading prices and making purchases require an App Store connection. If prices cannot be loaded, the support options remain visible but are unavailable for purchase. Check your connection and choose Retry. Editing does not require a support purchase or an internet connection.

Completed consumable tips have no feature entitlement to restore. Use your App Store purchase history to review charges or request purchase assistance.
